Director

Zhiyu Huang

Assistant Professor, North Carolina State University

Zhiyu Huang is an Assistant Professor at NC State and the Director of the PARIS Lab. Previously, he was a postdoctoral scholar at UCLA, a research intern at NVIDIA, and a visiting student researcher at UC Berkeley. He received his Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University (NTU).

His honors include the NTU MAE Best Ph.D. Thesis Award, an ICCV 2023 oral, the ITSC 2023 Best Paper Runner-up Award, the IEEE ITS Best Dissertation Award Finalist, and the Stanford/Elsevier Top 2% Scientists list.
