About the lab

Robots that see, think, and act

The PARIS Lab, directed by Prof. Zhiyu Huang, studies how to give robots the intelligence to work in the messy, dynamic real world. Our research sits at the intersection of robotics, machine learning, and embodied AI, with a focus on foundation models, efficient learning, and human-centered intelligence.

We validate our ideas on real hardware, from dual-arm manipulators and quadrupeds to autonomous vehicles.
